♟️ Controls & Gestures Guide

Quick reference for tabletop play on the Board

♟️ Tabletop Chess Gestures (Chess.com, Lichess, ChessKid & ChessReps)

Double-Tap & Drag

Double-tap on a starting square and drag across to draw live tactical arrows. Pieces on the origin square will not move.

Double-Tap in Place

Double-tap quickly on any square without dragging to toggle its color highlight.

Additive Markings

Multiple arrows and square highlights persist together on the board without disappearing as you draw additional lines.

Clear Markings

Tap any square once or make a piece move to immediately clear all arrows and square highlights.

1-Finger Piece Movement

Touch and drag any piece (> 10px) to move it normally with instant 0ms native responsiveness.

◫ Split Screen & Dual-App Multitasking

Tabletop Duos

Launch paired combinations from Launcher in 1 tap (Chess + YouTube, ChessKids + YouTube 70/30, Chess + Apple TV).

Center Divider Dragging

Drag the center divider thumb to resize panes from 20% to 80% with live snapping to 50/50, 70/30, and 30/70. Double-tap to reset to 50/50.

Independent Tabletop Padding ([ ◧ L-Pad ] / [ ◨ R-Pad ])

3-way cycle padding for each pane (Off → Outer Edge Inset → All 4 Sides Inset) to prevent bezel clipping on both apps.

Head-to-Head 180° Inversion (🔄 180°)

Invert the secondary pane 180° for opposite-seat tabletop players with hardware touch rotation.
